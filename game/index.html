SUNSET BAY

15 MISSIONS · 5-STAR COPS · CAR JACK · SHOPS · SAVE · RADIO · DAY-NIGHT

A sun-soaked coastal city of pastel towers, palm boulevards and one very borrowed car. Work the mission list, dodge the heat, be home by sunset.

W A S D — walk / drive
SHIFT — run
E — enter / exit car (jacking = +1 star)
F — interact in zones
SPACE — handbrake
S — save game
R — radio (after the tower)
M — mute
1 2 3 — buy in the shop
Goal — finish all 15 missions

One file. No downloads, no network — the whole city is drawn and beeped live. The safehouse clears your stars and heals you.
